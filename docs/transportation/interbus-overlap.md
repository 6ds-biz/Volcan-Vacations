Retrieved **2026-09-07** from [Interbus’s official route table](https://www.interbusonline.com/our-routes/). Only the existing RideCR canonical set was considered. No Interbus-only route was imported and no traversal of the full booking network was performed.

Match rules: preserve direction; normalize accents and the established Fortuna/La Fortuna alias; require a listed departure; do not infer a reverse leg. Ambiguous airport/city or broad-zone aliases require manual confirmation. Empty timetable cells are not evidence of a scheduled service.

**Six overlapping services; six schedule references.** Published durations are estimates, not exact arrival times. The table does not specify weekdays/effective dates, so these remain unknown and require operator recurrence entry before date-specific recommendation.

The remaining 18 canonical routes have no confirmed exact match in this review. This means **unverified overlap**, not proof Interbus never serves them. All are retained as RideCR-only in the import.

[Interbus’s FAQ](https://www.interbusonline.com/faqs/) discusses airport pickup, but that does not establish that a city-row departure time applies at the airport. Airport-city substitutions were therefore withheld. Sarapiquí is similarly broader than Puerto Viejo de Sarapiquí. Monteverde → La Fortuna is not listed as a matching row in the reviewed route table; La Fortuna → Monteverde does not prove its reverse. Future vendor confirmation can add a service to an existing canonical route without expanding the route universe.

Public consumer prices and general vehicle descriptions are not contracted VV net rates or guaranteed service capacity. Imported capacity and monetary rates remain empty.
